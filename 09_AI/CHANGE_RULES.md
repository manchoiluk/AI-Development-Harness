# Change Rules

Product change process:

```text
Change Request
      ↓
Impact Analysis
      ↓
Human Decision
      ↓
Update Product / Spec
      ↓
Update Design
      ↓
Create / Update Tasks
      ↓
Implementation
      ↓
Test
      ↓
Verification
```

> AI may propose a Change Proposal, but cannot approve product changes on its own.
